The problem

What happens when a Kafka cluster goes down

Consumers stop

The application is bound to one cluster. Moving to the standby means editing config and restarting by hand.

Producers lose messages

The send fails, and the handler often swallows the exception: the record is acknowledged but went nowhere.

Offsets don't match

The standby has offsets of its own. After a switch, records are skipped or processed twice.

Bridges between Kafkas break

A service moving data from one Kafka to another burns its retries in seconds and skips records.
