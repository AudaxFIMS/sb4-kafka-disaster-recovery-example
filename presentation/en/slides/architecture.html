Three roles. The health checker observes: every interval (5 s by default) it asks each cluster through an AdminClient. The manager decides: it counts consecutive failures and successes, elects the active cluster by priority, honours the failback-after window and persists its state. The executors act: consumer bindings are stopped on the old cluster and started on the new one, and the producer always writes to the active cluster. Replication is MirrorMaker 2 with IdentityReplicationPolicy, so topic names match.
