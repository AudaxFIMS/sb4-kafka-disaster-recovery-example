What the project gives you

Resilience by configuration. No DR code in the handlers

Automatic failover and failback — with a failback-after window that survives a restart

No gaps and no duplicates on the switch: seek by time plus idempotency

Several Kafkas in one service, and bridges between them without loss

Honest error classification: neither needless failovers nor silent loss

Dangerous settings don't start — the mistake shows at once, not in an incident

Extensible: your own idempotency, state and timestamp stores (Redis in the examples)

Backward compatible: single-group configurations work as before
