Data integrity

No gaps and no duplicates after the switch

Seek by time

On the new cluster reading starts at the timestamp of the last processed record — each consumer has its own.

Idempotency

Records at the switch boundary are dropped by key in an IdempotencyStore: in-memory or your own, Redis for one.

Watermark after commit

The watermark moves only once the offset is committed. With ack.owner: starter the starter commits — handlers need no acknowledgment code.

Three mechanisms work together. Seeking by time removes gaps: offsets differ between clusters, but MirrorMaker keeps record timestamps. The seek is approximate, so the boundary can repeat records; idempotency by message key drops them. Third, the watermark moves only after a real commit — otherwise a failover could jump past records that were never acknowledged.
