Bridges between Kafkas · depends-on

While the target Kafka is down, records wait instead of being lost

1 · Pause

The consumer's binding is paused while the dependency group has no healthy cluster. No rebalance.

2 · Gate

Records already polled never reach the handler: a nack, redelivered after an interval. No retries spent.

3 · Reclassify

A failed send inside the handler becomes a nack, not an exception. The idempotency mark is released.

max-hold

When the dependency is up yet the record still does not go through, it is held for at most depends-on-max-hold-ms (10 min). Time the group is down does not count.

A bridge is a consumer in one Kafka that writes to another. Without depends-on, the target Kafka going down looks like a handler error: the retries run out in seconds and the record is skipped. With depends-on there are three layers: pause, a gate before the handler, and reclassifying the failure. A nack does not count as a delivery attempt, so the wait can last as long as the outage. max-hold only bounds the case "the group is up but the record is not taken" — a missing ACL, for instance — which would otherwise stall the thread forever. A manual ack-mode is required and checked at startup.
