Summary

When the starter fits, and when it doesn't

A good fit

  • Spring Cloud Stream on Kafka, primary and standby clusters with MirrorMaker 2
  • Automatic failover is needed, without loss or duplicates
  • A service exchanges data between several Kafkas
  • Many teams need DR — done the same way

Not a fit

  • Active-active, or writing to several clusters at once
  • Strict exactly-once across Kafkas
  • Not Spring Cloud Stream: a plain Kafka client or Kafka Streams
  • A single cluster with no standby — the starter adds nothing

Kafka DR Starter

14

In short: the starter covers the typical active-passive DR scenario for Spring Cloud Stream, and exchange between independent Kafkas. Out of scope: active-active, exactly-once across clusters, other client stacks.
