The solution

The application writes ordinary handlers. Choosing the cluster, failing over, deduplicating and restoring the position is the starter's job — driven by configuration.

One YAML: clusters with priorities, consumers, producers. MirrorMaker 2 replicates the data between clusters.

The key message of the deck. The starter does not replace replication — MirrorMaker 2 does that. It answers the question "where do we read and write right now, and how do we avoid losing or duplicating data on the way over".
